Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy document contains how different types of information is used by the website.

1. Data Privacy Note

Should you choose to provide demographic information, a copy of the test results will be sent to us. We do NOT ever collect any identifying information. If you decide to skip the demographic information session, we do NOT see a copy of your scores. This is purely for data aggregation for our data explorer page.

2. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors engage with the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information and report website usage statistics without personally identifying individual visitors to Google.

The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps at www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/.

You can prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

4. Contact
